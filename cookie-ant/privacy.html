Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Cookie Ant is made by Go Dev. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything happens locally in your browser.

What the extension accesses

  • Cookies (cookies permission): the extension reads cookies only when you open it, to list them so you can choose what to export or copy. When you import a file, it writes only the cookies you select and confirm.
  • Site access (optional): Chrome only lets extensions read and write cookies for sites they may access. Cookie Ant asks for this only when you first use it, either for the current site or for all sites, and you can revoke it anytime in Chrome's extension settings. It never reads page content or injects scripts.
  • Active tab (activeTab permission): when you click the toolbar icon, the extension reads the address of the current tab to show quick actions for that site.
  • Storage (storage permission): keeps two values on your device only, how many exports and imports have succeeded and whether you dismissed the rating reminder. No cookie data is stored.
  • Files you import: the JSON or cookies.txt file you choose is read in memory on your device and is never uploaded. Exports are saved through Chrome's normal download flow.
  • Clipboard: when you choose "Copy as Cookie header" or "Copy as curl command", the text is written to your clipboard. The extension never reads your clipboard.

What the extension does not do

  • It makes no network requests and has no servers, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It does not store your cookies or files anywhere other than the export file you choose to save.
  • It does not sell or transfer data to third parties, does not use data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose, and does not use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Your responsibility

Exported cookie files can contain active login sessions. Keep them private, do not share them, and delete them when you no longer need them. Only import files you trust.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. Questions: nihao.godev@gmail.com

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 25 日

Cookie Ant 由 Go Dev 开发。它不收集、不传输、不出售、不共享你的任何数据。所有操作都在你的浏览器本地完成。

扩展会访问的内容

  • Cookie（cookies 权限）：仅在你打开扩展时读取 Cookie，供你选择要导出或复制的内容；导入时只写入你选择并确认的 Cookie。
  • 网站访问权限（可选）：Chrome 只允许扩展读写已授权网站的 Cookie。Cookie Ant 只在你首次使用时请求授权，可以只授权当前网站，也可以授权所有网站，并可随时在 Chrome 扩展设置中撤销。它从不读取网页内容，也不注入脚本。
  • 当前标签页（activeTab 权限）：点击工具栏图标时读取当前标签页的地址，以显示该网站的快捷操作。
  • 存储（storage 权限）：仅在本机保存两个值，即成功导出/导入的次数，以及你是否关闭了评分提示。不保存任何 Cookie 数据。
  • 你导入的文件：所选 JSON 或 cookies.txt 文件只在本机内存中读取，绝不上传。导出文件通过 Chrome 正常的下载流程保存。
  • 剪贴板：当你选择"复制为 Cookie 请求头"或"复制为 curl 命令"时，文本会写入剪贴板。扩展从不读取你的剪贴板。

扩展不会做的事

  • 不发起任何网络请求，没有服务器、统计分析、广告或追踪。
  • 除了你主动保存的导出文件外，不在任何地方存储你的 Cookie 或文件。
  • 不向第三方出售或转让数据，不将数据用于与单一用途无关的目的，也不用于信用评估或借贷。

使用提醒

导出的 Cookie 文件可能包含仍然有效的登录状态。请妥善保管、不要分享，并在不再需要时删除。只导入你信任的文件。
